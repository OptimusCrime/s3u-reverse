Whole-game evidence of verified functions

Every verified function passed its differential tests, usually also an emulator spec. Whole-game evidence means the game also called our replacement during a whole-game check (a recorded game replayed, or a savegame run) whose trace matched the original's (re/whole_game_calls.csv). "Tests only": installed and counted, but never called in any check. "May run inside": calls that come straight from our own replacements of the listed functions skip the counter, so these may have run anyway.
